TROUBLESHOOTING
=============================================================================

If you encounter issues:

1. Check pod events:
   kubectl describe pod -l app.kubernetes.io/instance={{ .Release.Name }}

2. Verify ConfigMaps:
   kubectl get configmap {{ include "flamoral.fullname" . }}-api-gateway-config

3. Check service endpoints:
   kubectl get endpoints -l app.kubernetes.io/instance={{ .Release.Name }}

4. Review ingress configuration:
   kubectl describe ingress {{ include "flamoral.fullname" . }}
