# Google Play Store Content Rating Questionnaire

## Overview

The Google Play Store requires all apps to complete a content rating questionnaire through the International Age Rating Coalition (IARC) system. This document contains the recommended answers for Flamoral based on its features and content.

**Expected Rating: MATURE 17+ / PEGI 18**

Due to:
- Dating and romantic content
- User-generated content
- Social networking features
- User communication features

---

## IARC Questionnaire Answers

### Category 1: Violence

**Q: Does your app contain any of the following?**

- [ ] Depictions of realistic violence toward realistic human or animal characters
- [ ] Depictions of death of realistic human or animal characters
- [ ] Depictions of dismemberment or mutilation of realistic human or animal characters
- [ ] Depictions of blood or gore

**Answer: NO to all**

**Explanation:** Flamoral is a dating app with no violent content.

---

### Category 2: Sexuality

**Q: Does your app contain any of the following?**

- [ ] References to or depictions of sexual content
- [ ] Nudity or partial nudity
- [ ] References to sexuality or sexual activity
- [X] Content related to sex or dating

**Answer: YES to "Content related to sex or dating"**

**Explanation:** Flamoral is explicitly a dating app designed to help people find romantic and intimate relationships. While we have strict policies against explicit sexual content, the app's purpose is dating and relationship formation.

## Age Rating Justification

### Target Audience: 18+

**Primary Reasons:**
1. **Dating Content:** App's purpose is romantic/intimate relationships
2. **User Communication:** Direct messaging between users
3. **User-Generated Content:** Photos, videos, messages
4. **Mature Themes:** Dating, relationships, romantic content
5. **Legal Requirements:** Age verification for dating services

### Enforcement:

- Hard age gate at registration (18+)
- Date of birth verification required
- Government ID verification for photo verification
- Account termination for underage users
- Age verification checks at multiple points
- Compliance with COPPA and international regulations

## Questionnaire Completion Steps

1. **Login to Google Play Console**
2. **Navigate to:** App Content > Content Ratings
3. **Start Questionnaire**
4. **Select Email:** privacy@flamoral.com
5. **Answer Questions** using this guide
6. **Review Age Ratings**
7. **Publish Ratings**

**Timeline:** Ratings are generated immediately upon questionnaire completion.
