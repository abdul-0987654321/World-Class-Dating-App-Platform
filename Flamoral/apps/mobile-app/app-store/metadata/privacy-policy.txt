FLAMORAL PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: December 11, 2025

INTRODUCTION

Flamoral ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you use our dating application.

INFORMATION WE COLLECT

Information You Provide:
- Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, date of birth, gender
- Profile Information: Photos, bio, interests, lifestyle preferences, location
- User Content: Messages, likes, matches, reports
- Payment Information: Processed securely through Apple In-App Purchase (we do not store payment details)

Information We Collect Automatically:
- Device Information: Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers
- Usage Data: App features used, interactions, time spent
- Location Data: Approximate location (city-level) for matching purposes
- Camera/Photo Access: For profile photos and verification (with your permission)

HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

We use your information to:
- Provide and improve our dating services
- Show you potential matches based on preferences and location
- Enable messaging between matched users
- Verify your identity and prevent fraud
- Process payments for premium features
- Send important service notifications
- Comply with legal obligations

DATA SHARING

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with:
- Other Users: Your profile information is visible to potential matches
- Service Providers: Cloud storage, communication services, payment processing
- Law Enforcement: When required by law or to protect safety

YOUR RIGHTS

You have the right to:
- Access your personal data
- Correct inaccurate information
- Delete your account and data
- Export your data
- Withdraw consent for data processing
- Opt out of marketing communications

ACCOUNT DELETION

You can delete your account anytime through:
1. App Settings > Account > Delete Account
2. Email: privacy@flamoral.com

All your data will be permanently deleted within 30 days.

DATA SECURITY

We use industry-standard security measures including:
- End-to-end encryption for messages
- Secure HTTPS connections
- Regular security audits
- Encrypted cloud storage
- Automated content moderation

CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Flamoral is only for users 18 years and older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

DATA RETENTION

- Active accounts: Data retained while account is active
- Deleted accounts: Data deleted within 30 days
- Legal requirements: Some data may be retained longer if required by law

INTERNATIONAL DATA TRANSFERS

Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place.

COOKIES AND TRACKING

We use essential cookies for authentication and app functionality. We do not use third-party advertising cookies or tracking.

CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this privacy policy. We'll notify you of significant changes through the app or email.

CONTACT US

For privacy questions or to exercise your rights:
- Email: privacy@flamoral.com
- Support: support@flamoral.com
- Website: https://flamoral.com/privacy

PERMISSIONS EXPLAINED

Camera: To add photos to your profile and verify your identity
Photo Library: To select existing photos for your profile
Location: To show you potential matches nearby (approximate location only)
Notifications: To notify you about matches and messages (optional)
Microphone: For video calls with matches (optional)
Face ID: To securely unlock the app (optional)

DATA SAFETY INFORMATION

Data Collected:
- Personal info (name, email, phone, photos)
- Location (approximate)
- Messages and interactions
- Payment information (processed by Apple)

Data Security:
- Encrypted in transit and at rest
- Regular security audits
- Secure authentication

Data Deletion:
- Users can delete accounts anytime
- All data permanently removed within 30 days
