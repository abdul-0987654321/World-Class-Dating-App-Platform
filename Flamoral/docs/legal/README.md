**When updating legal documents:**

1. **Create new version**

## Compliance Status

### Current Compliance

✅ **GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation)**
- Applies to: EU, EEA, UK users
- Status: Compliant
- Key features: User consent, data portability, right to deletion

✅ **CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act)**
- Applies to: California residents
- Status: Compliant
- Key features: Data access, deletion, opt-out of sale

✅ **COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act)**
- Applies to: Users under 13
- Status: Compliant (platform is 18+ only)

✅ **CAN-SPAM Act**
- Applies to: Email communications
- Status: Compliant
- Key features: Unsubscribe option, sender identification

✅ **DMCA (Digital Millennium Copyright Act)**
- Applies to: Copyright protection
- Status: Compliant
- Key features: Takedown procedures, safe harbor

✅ **TCPA (Telephone Consumer Protection Act)**
- Applies to: SMS/phone communications
- Status: Compliant
- Key features: Opt-in for SMS, opt-out available

## Best Practices for Updates

### Communication Best Practices

**For Material Changes:**
1. **Advance notice:** 30 days before effective date
2. **Multiple channels:** Email + in-app + website banner
3. **Summary of changes:** Plain language explanation
4. **Full document:** Link to complete updated policy
5. **Re-acceptance:** Require user to accept new terms

**For Minor Changes:**
1. **Notice:** Update "Last Updated" date
2. **Optional notification:** In-app message or changelog
3. **No re-acceptance required**

### User-Friendly Practices

- **Plain language:** Avoid legalese where possible
- **Table of contents:** For long documents
- **Summaries:** Key points at the beginning
- **Examples:** Illustrate complex concepts
- **Definitions:** Define technical or legal terms
- **FAQs:** Address common questions
- **Contact info:** Clear, accessible support

## Emergency Procedures

### In Case of Legal Challenge

1. **Immediately notify:**
   - Legal team (legal@flamoral.com)
   - Executive team
   - Insurance provider

2. **Preserve evidence:**
   - Don't delete relevant documents
   - Save all communications
   - Document timeline

3. **Consult counsel:**
   - Engage outside counsel if needed
   - Follow legal advice
   - Don't make public statements without approval

### In Case of Regulatory Inquiry

1. **Designate point of contact**
2. **Gather requested information**
3. **Respond within required timeframe**
4. **Cooperate fully**
5. **Document all interactions**

### In Case of Data Breach

See `SECURITY_INCIDENT_RESPONSE.md` (if available) or contact:
- Security team: security@flamoral.com
- DPO: dpo@flamoral.com
- Legal: legal@flamoral.com

**Timeline:**
- Notify users: Within 72 hours (GDPR requirement)
- Notify authorities: Within 72 hours (GDPR requirement)
- Public disclosure: As required by law
