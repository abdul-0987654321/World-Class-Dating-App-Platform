## Welcome to the Flamoral Community

At Flamoral, we're building a community where people can connect authentically and safely. These Community Guidelines outline our expectations for behavior on the platform and help create a positive experience for everyone.

By using Flamoral, you agree to follow these guidelines. Violations may result in warnings, content removal, account suspension, or permanent ban.

---

## Core Values

Our community is built on these principles:

- **Authenticity:** Be yourself and represent yourself honestly
- **Respect:** Treat others with kindness and dignity
- **Safety:** Help us maintain a safe environment for all users
- **Inclusivity:** Welcome people of all backgrounds, identities, and orientations
- **Consent:** Respect boundaries and communication preferences

---

## 1. Be Authentic

### 1.1 Use Real Information

- Use your real name and actual photos
- Provide accurate information about your age, location, and interests
- Do not impersonate others or create fake profiles
- Do not use photos of celebrities, models, or other people

### 1.2 One Account Per Person

- Maintain only one account at a time
- Do not create multiple accounts to evade bans or restrictions
- If you need to create a new account, delete your old one first

### 1.3 Verify Your Identity

- Complete photo verification when prompted
- Provide requested documentation if needed for verification
- Verified badges help others trust you're real

---

## 2. Respectful Behavior

### 2.1 Treat Others with Respect

**Do:**
- Be kind, courteous, and considerate
- Respect different opinions, backgrounds, and perspectives
- Use appropriate language
- Accept rejection gracefully
- Give compliments that are genuine and appropriate

**Don't:**
- Harass, bully, or intimidate others
- Use offensive, degrading, or discriminatory language
- Make unwanted sexual advances
- Continue contact after someone has said no
- Body shame or criticize others' appearance

### 2.2 No Hate Speech or Discrimination

We have zero tolerance for:
- Racism, sexism, homophobia, transphobia, or other discrimination
- Content that promotes hatred against any group
- Slurs or derogatory terms
- Content that advocates violence or harm
- Symbols or imagery associated with hate groups

### 2.3 Respect Boundaries

- Accept "no" for an answer
- Don't pressure others for personal information
- Don't ask for explicit photos or content
- Respect communication preferences
- Stop messaging if someone doesn't respond or asks you to stop

---

## 3. Photo and Content Guidelines

### 3.1 Profile Photos

**Photos Must:**
- Clearly show your face
- Be of you (not someone else)
- Be recent (within the past 2 years)
- Be appropriate for a public dating platform
- Be well-lit and clear

**Photos Cannot:**
- Contain nudity or sexually explicit content
- Show children (even if they're your own)
- Include weapons, drugs, or illegal items
- Be screenshots or heavily filtered/edited images
- Contain contact information or URLs
- Be promotional or commercial in nature
- Include text overlays (except minimal watermarks)

### 3.2 Additional Photos

- All additional photos must follow the same guidelines
- Group photos are acceptable but should not be all your photos
- Photos with pets are welcome
- Travel and hobby photos help showcase your personality

### 3.3 Profile Bio and Prompts

**Your bio should:**
- Describe your interests, hobbies, and personality
- Be honest and authentic
- Be written in a respectful manner

**Your bio cannot contain:**
- Offensive, explicit, or inappropriate content
- Contact information (phone, email, social media handles)
- Links to external websites
- Solicitation for services or products
- Discriminatory preferences based on race, religion, or other protected characteristics

### 3.4 Shared Content

When sharing content in messages:
- Do not send unsolicited explicit photos
- Do not share others' private information or photos without consent
- Do not send spam or promotional content
- Do not share violent, graphic, or disturbing content

## 4. Safe Communication

### 4.1 First Messages

- Start with a friendly, appropriate greeting
- Reference something from their profile
- Don't use copy-paste pickup lines excessively
- Avoid overly sexual or forward messages
- Don't ask for personal information immediately

### 4.2 Ongoing Conversations

**Good conversation practices:**
- Be genuine and interested
- Ask questions and engage meaningfully
- Respect response times (people have lives)
- Keep the conversation balanced
- Move to video chat or meeting in person when both ready

**Avoid:**
- Sending excessive messages if they don't respond
- Being pushy about meeting, personal information, or moving off-platform
- Making everything about physical appearance or sex
- Sending the same message to multiple people visibly
- Dominating the conversation

### 4.3 Moving Off-Platform

- Only share personal contact information when you're comfortable
- Don't feel pressured to give your phone number, social media, or other contacts
- Report users who aggressively push for personal information
- Be cautious about moving conversations to other platforms too quickly

## 5. Safety First

### 5.1 Protect Your Personal Information

**Don't share:**
- Home or work address
- Financial information
- ID numbers or documents
- Passwords or account access
- Intimate photos

**Be cautious sharing:**
- Phone number
- Full name
- Social media profiles
- Specific location details
- Work information

### 5.2 Meeting in Person

If you decide to meet someone from Flamoral:

**Before Meeting:**
- Get to know them through messages and video chat first
- Tell a friend or family member where you're going
- Share details about who you're meeting
- Plan to check in with someone during or after

**For the First Meeting:**
- Meet in a public place
- Arrange your own transportation
- Keep your phone charged
- Don't leave drinks unattended
- Trust your instincts
- Stay sober
- Don't feel obligated to continue if uncomfortable

**Red Flags:**
- They refuse to video chat before meeting
- They pressure you to meet quickly
- They suggest meeting at their home or your home
- They ask for money
- They avoid answering questions about themselves
- Your gut tells you something is off

### 5.3 Financial Safety

**Never:**
- Send money to someone you met on Flamoral
- Share bank account, credit card, or payment app details
- Invest in opportunities presented by matches
- Help someone with "financial emergencies"
- Buy gift cards or cryptocurrency for someone

**Common Scams:**
- Romance scams (building trust then asking for money)
- Investment scams
- Emergency scams ("I'm stuck in another country")
- Fake verification requests asking for payment

### 5.4 Report Suspicious Behavior

Report immediately if someone:
- Asks for money or financial help
- Pressures you for explicit content
- Threatens or blackmails you
- Shares explicit content without consent
- Appears to be underage
- Uses fake photos (reverse image search)
- Behaves aggressively or erratically

## 6. Prohibited Content and Activities

### 6.1 Explicit Sexual Content

**Prohibited:**
- Nudity or sexually explicit photos
- Pornographic content
- Sexual services or solicitation
- Unsolicited sexual messages or photos
- Explicit sexual content in profile or bio

### 6.2 Illegal Activities

**Prohibited:**
- Drugs or controlled substances
- Weapons, firearms, or explosives
- Illegal goods or services
- Human trafficking
- Prostitution or escort services
- Underage users or content
- Fraud or scams

### 6.3 Spam and Commercial Activity

**Prohibited:**
- Advertising products or services
- Promoting businesses or websites
- Affiliate marketing
- Promoting other social media accounts for followers
- Recruiting for MLM schemes
- Asking for donations or crowdfunding

### 6.4 Harassment and Abuse

**Prohibited:**
- Stalking or following someone on/off platform
- Doxing (sharing private information)
- Revenge porn or non-consensual intimate images
- Threats of violence or harm
- Blackmail or extortion
- Catfishing or deceptive practices

### 6.5 Minors

**Strictly Prohibited:**
- Users under 18 years old
- Sexualization of minors
- Soliciting contact with minors
- Sharing or requesting content depicting minors

Any content involving minors will be reported to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and law enforcement.

### 6.6 Self-Harm and Dangerous Content

**Prohibited:**
- Content promoting self-harm or suicide
- Eating disorder promotion
- Graphic violence or gore
- Content glorifying dangerous activities

If you're struggling, please reach out:
- National Suicide Prevention Lifeline: 988
- Crisis Text Line: Text HOME to 741741

## 7. Reporting and Enforcement

### 7.1 How to Report

If you see something that violates these guidelines:

**In the App:**
1. Go to the user's profile
2. Tap the menu icon (•••)
3. Select "Report"
4. Choose the reason for reporting
5. Provide additional details if needed

**Report the Following Immediately:**
- Underage users
- Threats or harassment
- Scams or fraud
- Explicit content sent without consent
- Safety concerns

### 7.2 What Happens After You Report

1. **Immediate Action:** Serious violations may result in immediate account suspension
2. **Review:** Our trust and safety team reviews all reports
3. **Investigation:** We investigate reports thoroughly and may request additional information
4. **Decision:** We determine if guidelines were violated
5. **Enforcement:** Appropriate action is taken (warning, content removal, suspension, or ban)
6. **Notification:** The user is notified of violations (except in safety cases)

### 7.3 Report Review Timeline

- High priority reports (safety, minors, threats): Within 24 hours
- Standard reports: Within 72 hours
- Complex cases: Up to 7 days

### 7.4 Enforcement Actions

Depending on the severity and frequency of violations:

**Warning:**
- First-time minor violations
- Notification of what was violated
- Guidance on proper behavior

**Content Removal:**
- Violating photos, messages, or profile content removed
- User notified and warned

**Temporary Suspension:**
- Access restricted for 24 hours to 30 days
- Serious or repeat violations
- Time to reflect and review guidelines

**Permanent Ban:**
- Severe violations (illegal content, threats, harassment)
- Repeated violations after warnings
- Ban on creating new accounts
- May be reported to law enforcement

### 7.5 Appeals

If you believe enforcement action was taken in error:

1. Email appeals@flamoral.com
2. Include your user ID and details
3. Explain why you believe the action was incorrect
4. We'll review and respond within 7 days

Appeals are not guaranteed to result in reinstatement.

### 7.6 False Reports

Malicious or false reporting is prohibited and may result in account suspension. Report features should only be used for genuine violations.
