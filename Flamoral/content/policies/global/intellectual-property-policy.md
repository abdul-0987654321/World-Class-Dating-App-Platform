## 4. DMCA Copyright Infringement Policy

### 4.1 Overview

Flamoral complies with the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA), 17 U.S.C. § 512.

**We Respond to:**
- Valid DMCA takedown notices
- Counter-notices from users
- Repeat infringer claims

### 4.2 Filing a DMCA Takedown Notice

**If Your Copyright Is Infringed:**

Send a written notice to our designated Copyright Agent with:

**Required Information:**

1. **Your Signature:** Physical or electronic signature of copyright owner or authorized agent

2. **Identification of Copyrighted Work:**
   - Description of copyrighted work claimed to be infringed
   - If multiple works, a representative list

3. **Identification of Infringing Material:**
   - Description of material claimed to be infringing
   - Specific location (URL, user profile, etc.)
   - Sufficient detail for us to locate it

4. **Your Contact Information:**
   - Name, address, telephone number, email address

5. **Statement of Good Faith:**
   - "I have a good faith belief that use of the material in the manner complained of is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law."

6. **Statement of Accuracy:**
   - "The information in this notification is accurate, and under penalty of perjury, I am the owner, or authorized to act on behalf of the owner, of an exclusive right that is allegedly infringed."

**Send Notice To:**

**Email (Preferred):** dmca@flamoral.com
**Subject Line:** "DMCA Takedown Notice"

**Mail:**
Flamoral Inc.
DMCA Copyright Agent
[Address TBD]

**Response Time:** We aim to respond within 2-5 business days.

### 4.3 What Happens After Notice

**We Will:**

1. **Review Notice:** Verify it meets DMCA requirements
2. **Take Action:** If valid:
   - Remove or disable access to allegedly infringing content
   - Notify the user who posted it
   - Provide copy of notice to user (with your contact info)
3. **Track Violations:** Note infringement on user's account
4. **Terminate Repeat Infringers:** Per our policy (see 4.6)

**We May:**
- Request additional information
- Reject incomplete or invalid notices
- Forward notice to Lumen Database (public archive)

### 4.4 Filing a DMCA Counter-Notice

**If Your Content Was Removed:**

If you believe removal was erroneous, file a counter-notice with:

**Required Information:**

1. **Your Signature:** Physical or electronic

2. **Identification of Material:**
   - Description of removed content
   - Location before removal

3. **Statement Under Penalty of Perjury:**
   - "I swear, under penalty of perjury, that I have a good faith belief that the material was removed or disabled as a result of mistake or misidentification of the material to be removed or disabled."

4. **Consent to Jurisdiction:**
   - "I consent to the jurisdiction of Federal District Court for the judicial district in which my address is located (or [District TBD] if outside the U.S.), and I will accept service of process from the person who filed the DMCA notice or their agent."

5. **Your Contact Information:**
   - Name, address, telephone number, email

**Send Counter-Notice To:**

**Email:** dmca@flamoral.com
**Subject:** "DMCA Counter-Notice"

**What Happens:**

1. **We Review:** Verify counter-notice meets requirements
2. **We Notify Complainant:** Forward your counter-notice
3. **Wait Period:** 10-14 business days
4. **Reinstate Content:** If complainant doesn't file court action
   - OR we keep it removed if they sue

### 4.5 False Claims

**Warning:** Filing false DMCA notices or counter-notices may result in:
- Liability for damages (including costs and attorney's fees)
- Perjury charges
- Account termination
- Legal action

**Only File If:**
- You genuinely own copyright (for takedowns)
- You genuinely believe removal was mistake (for counter-notices)
- You understand legal consequences of false statements

### 4.6 Repeat Infringer Policy

**We Terminate Accounts of Repeat Infringers.**

**Repeat Infringer Defined:**
- User with multiple valid DMCA notices (typically 3+)
- User repeatedly posting infringing content
- User with pattern of infringement

**Process:**
1. **First Violation:** Warning + content removal
2. **Second Violation:** Temporary suspension + final warning
3. **Third Violation:** Permanent account termination

**Exceptions:**
- Consideration for counter-notices
- Review of circumstances
- Appeals process available

**Appeals:**
- Email: appeals@flamoral.com
- Include: account info, reason, supporting evidence
- Decision final after review

### 4.7 Other Copyright Issues

**For Non-DMCA Copyright Issues:**
- Outside U.S. copyright claims
- General IP questions
- Licensing inquiries

**Contact:** legal@flamoral.com
