## Plain Language Summary

**What this means for you:**

Flamoral is a dating platform that helps you connect with meaningful relationships. We collect information you provide (like your profile, photos, and preferences) and information about how you use our service. We use this to:

- Match you with compatible people
- Keep our community safe
- Improve our service

**Your rights:** You can access, download, correct, or delete your data at any time. You can control who sees your profile and how we use your information.

**We never sell your personal data.** We only share it with service providers who help us run the platform, and only when required by law.

**We never sell your personal data.** We only share information in the following circumstances:

**You Control:**

**Communication Services:**

**Customer Support:**
- Help desk software

All service providers are bound by confidentiality agreements and can only use your data as instructed by us.

### 4.3 For Legal Reasons

We may disclose information when:
- Required by law, regulation, or legal process

- Protecting rights, property, or safety of Flamoral, users, or the public
- Detecting, preventing, or addressing fraud or security issues

### 4.4 Business Transfers

If Flamoral is involved in a merger, acquisition, bankruptcy, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred. We will notify you before your information is transferred and becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

### 4.5 With Your Consent

We may share information with third parties when you explicitly consent, such as:
- Sharing your profile with a specific person
- Participating in surveys or research
- Using social sharing features

### 4.6 Aggregated and Anonymized Data

We may share aggregated, de-identified data that cannot reasonably identify you:
- Usage statistics and trends

## 5. Data Retention

### 5.1 Active Accounts

We retain your data while your account is active to provide the Service.

### 5.2 Deleted Accounts

When you delete your account:
- Your profile is removed from the Service within 24 hours
- We retain some data for legitimate purposes:
  - **90 days:** Messages and interactions (for safety investigations)
  - **1 year:** Payment records (for tax and accounting)
  - **2 years:** Safety reports and moderation records (to prevent ban evasion)
  - **As required by law:** Data subject to legal holds or investigations

### 5.3 Marketing Data

If you opt out of marketing, we retain your email on a suppression list indefinitely to honor your preference.

### 5.4 Inactive Accounts

Accounts inactive for 2+ years may be deleted after notice to your registered email.
