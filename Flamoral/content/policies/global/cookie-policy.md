## 1. Introduction

This Cookie Policy explains how Flamoral Inc. ("Flamoral," "we," "us," or "our") uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on our website, mobile applications, and related services (collectively, the "Service").

This policy should be read together with our [Privacy Policy](./privacy-policy.md).

### 1.1 What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device (computer, smartphone, tablet) when you visit a website or use an app. They help websites remember information about your visit.

### 1.2 Similar Technologies

We also use similar technologies:
- **Pixels/Web Beacons:** Tiny images that track user behavior
- **Local Storage:** Browser storage for data persistence
- **Session Storage:** Temporary storage for session data
- **SDKs:** Software development kits in mobile apps
- **Device Fingerprinting:** Identifying devices by their characteristics

When we say "cookies" in this policy, we generally mean all these technologies.

## 2. Types of Cookies We Use

### 2.1 By Duration

**Session Cookies:**
- Temporary cookies
- Deleted when you close your browser
- Used for navigation and functionality

**Persistent Cookies:**
- Remain on your device for set period
- Stored until expiration or manual deletion
- Used for preferences and analytics

### 2.2 By Purpose

We categorize cookies into four types:

#### A. Essential/Strictly Necessary Cookies

**Purpose:** Required for the Service to function
**Can Be Disabled:** No (required for basic operation)

**We Use Them To:**
- Authenticate users and maintain login sessions
- Enable core platform features
- Remember your privacy preferences
- Ensure security and prevent fraud
- Load balance across servers
- Enable payment processing

**Examples:**
- `session_id` - Maintains your login session
- `csrf_token` - Protects against cross-site request forgery
- `cookie_consent` - Remembers your cookie preferences
- `device_id` - Identifies your device for security

**Duration:** Session to 1 year

**Legal Basis:** Necessary for contract performance

#### B. Functional/Preference Cookies

**Purpose:** Remember your choices and personalize experience
**Can Be Disabled:** Yes

**We Use Them To:**
- Remember your language preference
- Store your filter and search preferences
- Remember notification settings
- Save your display preferences (light/dark mode)
- Personalize content and recommendations

**Examples:**
- `language` - Your preferred language
- `filters` - Saved search filters
- `display_mode` - Light or dark theme
- `notification_prefs` - Notification preferences

**Duration:** 30 days to 2 years

**Legal Basis:** Consent (EU/UK), Legitimate Interest (other regions)

#### C. Analytics/Performance Cookies

**Purpose:** Understand how you use the Service
**Can Be Disabled:** Yes

**We Use Them To:**
- Count visitors and measure traffic
- See which features are most popular
- Understand user journeys through the app
- Identify technical issues
- Measure performance and load times
- Conduct A/B tests

**Examples:**
- `_ga` - Google Analytics identifier
- `_gid` - Google Analytics session identifier
- `mixpanel` - Mixpanel analytics
- `amplitude` - Amplitude analytics
- `ab_test_variant` - A/B test assignment

**Duration:** Session to 2 years

**Legal Basis:** Consent (EU/UK), Legitimate Interest (other regions)

**Third Parties:**
- Google Analytics
- Mixpanel
- Amplitude
- Hotjar (heatmaps and session recordings)

#### D. Advertising/Marketing Cookies

**Purpose:** Show relevant advertisements
**Can Be Disabled:** Yes

**We Use Them To:**
- Deliver personalized ads
- Limit ad frequency
- Measure ad effectiveness
- Track conversions
- Build user profiles for advertising

**Examples:**
- `_fbp` - Facebook Pixel
- `_gcl_au` - Google AdSense
- `NID` - Google Ads
- `personalization_id` - Twitter advertising

**Duration:** Session to 2 years

**Legal Basis:** Consent

**Third Parties:**
- Google Ads
- Facebook Ads
- Twitter Ads
- LinkedIn Ads
- Retargeting platforms

## 6. Impact of Blocking Cookies

### 6.1 Essential Cookies

**If You Block Essential Cookies:**
- Cannot log in or create account
- Cannot use core features
- Security protections disabled
- Payment processing unavailable

**We Recommend:** Keep essential cookies enabled.

### 6.2 Functional Cookies

**If You Block Functional Cookies:**
- Must set preferences each visit
- Loss of personalization
- Repetitive selections required
- Reduced convenience

**Impact:** Moderate inconvenience.

### 6.3 Analytics Cookies

**If You Block Analytics Cookies:**
- No impact on your experience
- Helps you maintain privacy
- We receive less usage data

**Impact:** None on your experience.

### 6.4 Advertising Cookies

**If You Block Advertising Cookies:**
- Still see ads (but less relevant)
- Ads may repeat more often
- No impact on free features

**Impact:** Less personalized ads.

## Appendix A: Technical Details

### A.1 Cookie Attributes

We use these cookie attributes for security:

**Secure:** Only transmitted over HTTPS
**HttpOnly:** Not accessible via JavaScript (prevents XSS)
**SameSite:** Controls cross-site sending
- `Strict`: Never sent cross-site
- `Lax`: Sent on top-level navigation
- `None`: Always sent (requires Secure)

### A.2 Storage Mechanisms

**We Use:**
- **HTTP Cookies:** Traditional browser cookies
- **Local Storage:** Persistent browser storage (no expiration)
- **Session Storage:** Cleared when tab closes
- **IndexedDB:** Structured data storage
- **Cache Storage:** For offline functionality

### A.3 Mobile App Identifiers

**iOS:**
- IDFA (Identifier for Advertisers) - with permission
- IDFV (Identifier for Vendors) - automatic
- Keychain for secure storage

**Android:**
- Advertising ID - with permission
- Android ID - automatic
- Shared Preferences for storage
