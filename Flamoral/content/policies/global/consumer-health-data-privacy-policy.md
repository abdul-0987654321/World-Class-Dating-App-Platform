## Plain Language Summary

**What is consumer health data?**
Personal information about your physical or mental health, healthcare, or health-related activities.

**Health data we may collect:**
- Mental health and wellness information (if you share it)
- Relationship counseling preferences
- Health conditions affecting relationships (optional)

## 1. Introduction

This Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy explains how Flamoral Inc. ("Flamoral," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects consumer health data in compliance with applicable laws, including:
- Washington State My Health My Data Act (MHMDA)
- California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) - sensitive personal information provisions
- Nevada Revised Statutes Chapter 629
- Other applicable state and federal health privacy laws

This policy supplements our main [Privacy Policy](./privacy-policy.md) and applies specifically to consumer health data.

### 1.1 What is Consumer Health Data?

**Consumer Health Data** means personal information that identifies or can be linked to a consumer and:
- Relates to physical or mental health
- Relates to health conditions, treatment, diseases, or diagnosis
- Identifies healthcare providers or services
- Relates to reproductive or sexual health
- Relates to biometric data used for health purposes
- Relates to gender-affirming care or services
- Relates to bodily functions, vital signs, symptoms, or measurements
- Includes social, psychological, behavioral, and medical data for health assessment

**In Flamoral's Context, This May Include:**
- Mental health status or history
- Anxiety, depression, or other mental health conditions
- Therapy or counseling history
- Substance use or recovery information
- Sexual health status or history
- STI/STD status or testing
- Reproductive health information
- Medications affecting relationships
- Physical health conditions disclosed in profile
- Disability or accessibility needs
- Gender identity and transition information
- Body image or eating disorder history
- Wellness and fitness data shared on profile

### 1.2 Application of This Policy

This policy applies when you:
- Voluntarily share health information on your profile
- Use health-related features or questionnaires
- Disclose health data in conversations
- Participate in wellness programs or features
- Link health apps or wearables (if applicable)

**Important:** Flamoral is not a healthcare provider, and this is not a HIPAA-covered service.

## 2. Collection of Consumer Health Data

### 2.1 Data We Collect

**Voluntarily Provided Information:**

**Profile Information:**
- Mental health status (e.g., "I see a therapist")
- Physical health conditions affecting dating
- Medications or treatments
- Lifestyle health factors
- Wellness routines and habits

**Relationship Context:**
- Preferences regarding partners' health
- Health-related deal breakers
- Compatibility factors related to health

**Questionnaires and Assessments:**
- Mental health screening (if you opt in)
- Wellness assessments
- Relationship readiness questions
- Health-related matching preferences

**Communications:**
- Health information shared in messages
- Discussions about health with matches
- Health-related support requests

**Third-Party Integrations (Future):**
- Fitness app data (if you connect)
- Wellness tracker information
- Health app integrations (with permission)

### 2.2 Data We Do NOT Collect (Unless You Choose to Share)

**We Don't Require:**
- Medical diagnoses
- Detailed health history
- Prescription information
- Medical records
- Insurance information
- Healthcare provider details

**All health data sharing is optional and at your discretion.**

### 2.3 How We Collect

**Direct Collection:**
- Profile setup and customization
- Optional questionnaires
- In-app feature usage
- Customer support interactions
- Voluntary disclosures

**Indirect Collection:**
- Inferred from behavior (e.g., using accessibility features)
- Derived from other information (e.g., health-related interests)
- From matches or other users (e.g., reports about concerning behavior)

**We Always:**
- Obtain explicit consent before collecting sensitive health data
- Clearly disclose what we're collecting and why
- Provide options to skip or opt out
- Allow deletion of health data

### 2.4 Consent Requirements

**Before Collecting Health Data:**
- We provide clear notice of what we're collecting
- Explain how it will be used
- Obtain your affirmative consent
- Allow you to decline without penalty

**Consent Is:**
- Specific to each type of health data
- Freely given (optional, not required for basic service)
- Informed (we explain clearly)
- Unambiguous (requires affirmative action)
- Revocable (you can withdraw anytime)

**Separate Consent for:**
- Different categories of health data
- Different uses (e.g., matching vs. research)
- Sharing with third parties
- Marketing uses

**We Will NOT Use Health Data To:**
- Discriminate against you
- Deny service or features
- Increase pricing

- Make automated decisions affecting rights without human review

### 3.3 Specific Use Cases

- To understand user wellbeing (aggregated only)

**Sexual Health Information:**
- Used for matching based on sexual health priorities

**Physical Health/Disability:**

### 4.4 De-Identification

**For Research and Analytics:**
- We de-identify health data
- Remove direct identifiers
- Apply technical safeguards
- Aggregate data
- Cannot reasonably re-identify

**De-Identified Data:**
- Not subject to same restrictions
- Used for research and improvement

## 5. Security and Protection

### 5.1 Enhanced Security Measures

**Health Data Receives Additional Protection:**

**Encryption:**
- AES-256 encryption at rest
- TLS 1.3 in transit
- End-to-end encryption for sensitive communications
- Encrypted backups

**Access Controls:**
- Role-based access (strict need-to-know)
- Multi-factor authentication required
- Access logging and monitoring
- Annual access reviews
- Minimal number of personnel

**Technical Safeguards:**
- Separate databases for health data
- Additional firewalls
- Intrusion detection specific to health data
- Regular penetration testing

**Physical Security:**
- SOC 2 Type II certified data centers
- Physical access controls
- Geographic redundancy

### 5.2 Data Minimization

**We Collect Only:**
- Health data necessary for stated purpose
- With your explicit consent
- Least sensitive data that meets need

**We Don't:**
- Collect health data by default
- Require health data for basic service
- Retain longer than necessary

### 5.3 Breach Notification

**If Health Data Is Breached:**
- Notification within 60 days (or as required by law)
- Notice to affected individuals
- Notice to regulators (if required)
- Description of data involved
- Steps we're taking
- Steps you can take
- Contact information for questions

**Notice Methods:**
- Email to registered address
- In-app notification
- Website notice
- Other prominent methods

## 6. Your Rights and Choices

### 6.1 Access and Portability

**Right to Access:**
- Request copy of your health data
- See how it's been used
- Know who it's been shared with
- Receive in portable format

**How to Request:**
- Settings > Privacy > Download My Health Data
- Email: healthdata@flamoral.com
- Delivered within 45 days

### 6.2 Correction and Deletion

**Right to Correct:**
- Update inaccurate health information
- Modify your health data
- Request correction if wrong

**Right to Delete:**
- Request deletion of health data
- Remove health information from profile
- Withdraw from health-related features

**How to Exercise:**
- Settings > Privacy > Health Data > Delete
- Or email: healthdata@flamoral.com

**Note:** Some data retained for legal compliance or safety (will notify you)

### 6.3 Consent Withdrawal

**Right to Withdraw Consent:**
- Revoke permission for health data collection
- Opt out of health-related features
- Stop health data processing

**Effect of Withdrawal:**
- Future collection stops
- Health-based matching disabled
- Past data deleted (unless legally required to retain)
- No penalty to basic service

**How to Withdraw:**
- Settings > Privacy > Health Data > Withdraw Consent
- Or email: healthdata@flamoral.com
- Effective immediately

### 6.4 Objection and Restriction

**Right to Object:**
- Object to specific health data uses
- Object to health data processing
- Object to profiling or automated decisions

**Right to Restrict:**
- Limit how health data is used
- Restrict sharing with third parties
- Restrict certain processing activities

### 6.5 Non-Discrimination

**We Will NOT:**
- Deny you service for exercising rights
- Charge different prices
- Provide different quality of service
- Suggest you'll receive inferior service

**Your Rights Are Protected.**

## 7. State-Specific Requirements

### 7.1 Washington State (My Health My Data Act)

**Additional Rights for Washington Residents:**

**Geofencing Prohibition:**
- We don't use geofencing to identify or track at health facilities

**Consent Requirements:**
- Valid consent required for collection, sharing, and use
- Consent must be separate for each activity
- Clear and conspicuous notice

**Data Sharing Log:**
- Available upon request showing all entities receiving your health data

**Right to Appeal:**
- If request denied, appeal to Attorney General

**Contact:** Washington AG's Office if you have concerns

### 7.2 California (CCPA/CPRA)

**Sensitive Personal Information Protections:**

**Right to Limit:**
- Limit use of sensitive personal information (including health data)
- We only use for permitted purposes:
  - Providing services you request
  - Security and safety
  - Short-term, transient use
  - Quality control

**Right to Know:**
- Categories of sensitive data collected
- Purposes of use
- Whether sold or shared (we don't)

**How to Limit:**
- Settings > Privacy > Limit Use of Sensitive Data
- Link: "Limit the Use of My Sensitive Personal Information"

### 7.3 Nevada

**Nevada Health Data Protections:**

**Sale Prohibition:**
- We don't sell health data
- Nevada residents can opt out (even though we don't sell)

**Security Requirements:**
- Encryption of health data
- Notice of unauthorized access

### 7.4 Connecticut

**Connecticut Data Privacy Act:**
- Consent required for processing sensitive data
- Right to opt out of targeted advertising
- Data protection assessments for health data processing

### 7.5 Other States

As other states enact health data laws, we comply with applicable requirements. Contact healthdata@flamoral.com for state-specific questions.

## 9. Data Retention and Deletion

### 9.1 Retention Periods

**Active Account:**
- Health data retained while you consent and account active

**After Consent Withdrawal:**
- Deleted within 30 days
- Except data needed for legal compliance or safety

**After Account Deletion:**
- Health data deleted within 60 days
- Backups purged within 90 days
- Legal holds may extend retention

### 9.2 Deletion Exceptions

**We May Retain If:**
- Required by law
- Subject to legal hold or investigation
- Necessary for safety (e.g., crisis intervention record)
- De-identified for research (cannot identify you)

**We Will:**
- Notify you if we must retain
- Delete as soon as legal requirement ends
- Maintain minimum necessary

### 9.3 Secure Deletion

**When Deleting:**
- Overwrite data
- Purge from backups
- Remove from all systems
- Confirm deletion
- Provide confirmation upon request
