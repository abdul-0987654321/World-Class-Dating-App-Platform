UNIQUE FEATURES - WHAT DIFFERENTIATES FLAMORAL
============================================

Flamoral is not a generic dating app. It introduces several innovative features not found in competing apps:

1. AI CHEMISTRY PREDICTION
   - Proprietary algorithm predicts compatibility based on communication patterns
   - Shows "chemistry score" before matching to help users make informed decisions
   - Uses NLP analysis of profile responses (not just photos)

2. GHOSTING PREVENTION SYSTEM
   - AI monitors conversation patterns to detect fading interest
   - Sends gentle engagement prompts to both users
   - Match expiration timers encourage meaningful connections
   - Industry-first feature addressing #1 user complaint in dating

3. EMOTIONAL AVAILABILITY TRACKING
   - Users can set their current emotional state/dating readiness
   - Matches shown based on compatible availability levels
   - Reduces mismatched expectations (serious vs casual)

4. AI DATING COACH
   - Real-time conversation suggestions during chats
   - Personalized opening lines based on profile content
   - Helps users improve their dating communication skills

5. MEETING READINESS DETECTOR
   - AI determines when both users are ready to meet IRL
   - Suggests appropriate date ideas based on shared interests
   - Reduces anxiety around "when to ask to meet"

6. SEMANTIC MATCH SCORING
   - Goes beyond age/location to analyze values alignment
   - Uses profile essays and prompt responses for deeper matching
   - Shows specific compatibility reasons, not just a percentage

7. WOMEN-FIRST MESSAGING WITH AI ENHANCEMENT
   - In heterosexual matches, women message first
   - AI suggests quality opening messages
   - Reduces harassment and improves conversation quality

8. SPEED DATING EVENTS
   - Live virtual speed dating sessions
   - Timed video chats with multiple users
   - Creates urgency and real-time connections

9. TRAVEL MODE
   - Plan matches for future destinations
   - Connect with locals before traveling
   - Schedule availability for specific date ranges

10. GAMIFICATION & ACHIEVEMENTS
    - Rewards for meaningful engagement (not just swiping)
    - Streaks for consistent, quality conversations
    - Levels and badges for positive behaviors
