## Creating Your Account

### Step 1: Download the App
- **iOS**: Download Flamoral from the Apple App Store
- **Android**: Download Flamoral from the Google Play Store
- **Web**: Visit [flamoral.com](https://flamoral.com) in your browser

### Step 2: Sign Up
You have three options to create your account:

#### Option 1: Email Registration
1. Tap "Sign up with Email"
2. Enter your email address
3. Create a secure password (minimum 8 characters with uppercase, lowercase, and numbers)
4. Verify your email by clicking the link sent to your inbox

#### Option 2: Phone Number Registration
1. Tap "Sign up with Phone"
2. Enter your phone number
3. Verify with the SMS code sent to your phone
4. Create a password for your account

#### Option 3: Social Login
1. Choose one of the following:
   - Sign up with Google
   - Sign up with Facebook
   - Sign up with Apple
2. Authorize Flamoral to access your basic profile information
3. Complete any additional required information

### Step 3: Age Verification
- You must be 18 years or older to use Flamoral
- Provide your date of birth
- For enhanced safety, you may be asked to verify your age with ID

### Step 4: Location Setup
- Allow Flamoral to access your location
- This helps us show you potential matches nearby
- You can adjust your discovery radius in settings later

### Step 5: Notification Preferences
Choose which notifications you'd like to receive:
- New matches
- New messages
- Profile likes
- Daily match recommendations
- Special promotions

## Profile Setup Best Practices

Your profile is your first impression - make it count!

### Profile Photos (Required: Minimum 2, Recommended: 4-9)

#### Photo Guidelines
- **First Photo**: A clear, recent solo photo of your face
- **Mix it up**: Include a variety of photos showing:
  - Close-up face shots
  - Full-body photos
  - You doing activities you enjoy
  - Photos with friends (but make it clear which one is you)
  - Travel photos or interesting locations

#### Photo Do's and Don'ts
**DO:**
- Use recent photos (within the last year)
- Smile and look approachable
- Show your personality
- Use good lighting
- Include at least one full-body photo
- Show your hobbies and interests

**DON'T:**
- Use heavily filtered or edited photos
- Include group photos where you can't be identified
- Post photos with exes (crop them out)
- Use photos with sunglasses in every shot
- Include inappropriate or offensive content
- Use photos with children unless they're yours and you're comfortable sharing

### Writing Your Bio

#### Bio Best Practices
- **Length**: Aim for 200-500 characters
- **Be authentic**: Write in your own voice
- **Show, don't tell**: Instead of "I'm funny," share a funny anecdote
- **Include conversation starters**: Mention interests people can ask about
- **Be specific**: "I love hiking Runyon Canyon" is better than "I like outdoors"
- **Update regularly**: Refresh your bio to reflect current interests

#### Bio Examples
**Good Example:**
> "Coffee enthusiast by day, amateur chef by night. You'll find me trying new restaurants, hiking local trails, or at a jazz concert. Looking for someone to share good food, great conversations, and spontaneous adventures. Bonus points if you can recommend a good book!"

**Avoid:**
> "Looking for someone real. No drama. Swipe left if you can't handle honesty."

### Complete Your Profile Details

Fill out as much as possible to help potential matches get to know you:

#### Essential Information
- **Height**: Be honest - it helps set realistic expectations
- **Location**: Your city/area is visible to matches
- **Education**: Add your school or highest degree
- **Job Title**: Share what you do professionally
- **Company**: Optional, but helps with conversation starters

#### Lifestyle Preferences
- **Relationship Goal**: What you're looking for (relationship, casual dating, etc.)
- **Smoking**: Be honest about your habits
- **Drinking**: Social drinker, non-drinker, etc.
- **Exercise**: Activity level
- **Religion**: Optional, but helpful for compatibility
- **Political Views**: Optional

#### Interests and Hobbies
Select at least 5 interests from our list:
- Helps match with like-minded people
- Great conversation starters
- Shows personality beyond photos

### Profile Prompts (Optional but Recommended)

Answer 2-3 profile prompts to showcase your personality:
- "The key to my heart is..."
- "I'm weirdly attracted to..."
- "The one thing you should know about me is..."
- "My simple pleasures..."
- "I spend way too much time thinking about..."

These prompts give potential matches more to talk about than just your photos.

### Profile Verification (Highly Recommended)

Get the verified badge to increase trust:
1. Go to Profile Settings
2. Tap "Verify Your Profile"
3. Take a selfie following the on-screen instructions
4. Our AI will verify it matches your profile photos
5. Receive your verification badge within 24 hours

**Benefits of verification:**
- Verified profiles get 40% more matches
- Shows you're a real, trustworthy person
- Helps combat fake profiles and catfishing

## App Navigation Overview

### Main Tabs

#### 1. Discover (Swipe) Tab
- **What it is**: Your main swiping interface
- **How to use**:
  - Swipe right to like
  - Swipe left to pass
  - Swipe up for Super Like (limited per day)
  - Tap the profile card to view full profile
  - Tap the info icon (i) to see more details

**Discovery Filters:**
- Tap the filter icon to adjust:
  - Age range
  - Distance (miles/km)
  - Advanced filters (Premium only): height, education, religion, etc.

#### 2. Matches Tab
- **What it is**: View all your matches
- **Features**:
  - See who you've matched with
  - Top Picks: Daily curated matches based on your preferences
  - Likes You: See who liked you (Premium feature)
  - Recently Active: Matches who are currently online

#### 3. Messages Tab
- **What it is**: All your conversations in one place
- **Features**:
  - Active conversations
  - Archived conversations
  - Search messages
  - Unmatch or report users
  - Video/voice call buttons

#### 4. Profile Tab
- **What it is**: Your profile and settings
- **Quick Actions**:
  - Edit profile
  - Manage photos
  - Update preferences
  - Account settings
  - Safety center
  - Help and support

### Top Navigation Icons

#### Bell Icon (Notifications)
- New matches
- New messages
- Profile likes
- System announcements

#### Star Icon (Premium)
- Upgrade to Premium
- View subscription benefits
- Manage subscription
- Purchase boosts and Super Likes

## Tips for Success

### Getting More Matches

1. **Complete Your Profile**: Profiles with 6+ photos and a completed bio get 3x more matches
2. **Be Active**: Daily users get shown to more potential matches
3. **Update Your Photos**: Fresh photos every few months keep your profile current
4. **Use Super Likes Wisely**: Save them for profiles you really connect with
5. **Swipe During Peak Hours**: More users are active 6-9 PM weekdays and weekends

### Starting Great Conversations

1. **Read Their Profile**: Comment on something specific from their bio or photos
2. **Ask Open-Ended Questions**: Avoid yes/no questions
3. **Be Creative**: Move beyond "Hey" or "How are you?"
4. **Use Humor**: (When appropriate) Make them smile
5. **Be Timely**: Respond within 24 hours to keep momentum

**Good Conversation Starters:**
- "I see you're into hiking! What's your favorite trail?"
- "That photo from Japan is amazing! How long were you there?"
- "Fellow coffee lover here! Have you tried [local coffee shop]?"

### Safety Tips

1. **Keep Conversations on Flamoral**: Until you're comfortable, don't share personal contact info
2. **Trust Your Instincts**: If something feels off, it probably is
3. **Google Your Date**: It's okay to do a quick search before meeting
4. **Meet in Public**: First dates should always be in public places
5. **Tell a Friend**: Share your date plans with someone you trust
6. **Video Chat First**: Use Flamoral's video call feature before meeting in person

### Profile Optimization

**Weekly Profile Maintenance:**
- Check your profile from a match's perspective
- Update bio to reflect current interests
- Rotate photos (move your best performer to #1)
- Answer new profile prompts
- Update your preferences if they've changed

**Monthly Profile Refresh:**
- Add new recent photos
- Remove photos that aren't getting engagement
- Revise your bio
- Update your location if you've moved

## Common Questions

### How do I know if someone liked me?
- Free users see a blurred preview in the "Likes You" section
- Premium users can see exactly who liked them

### How many likes do I get per day?
- **Free**: 50 likes per day (resets at midnight)
- **Premium**: Unlimited likes

### What's the difference between a like and a Super Like?
- **Like**: Standard way to show interest
- **Super Like**: Sends a notification and shows them you're really interested
- Free users get 5 Super Likes per week
- Premium users get 5 per week, Premium+ users get 10 per week

### Can I undo a swipe?
- Free users cannot undo swipes
- Premium users get the "Rewind" feature to undo the last swipe
- Premium+ users get unlimited rewinds

### How far can I change my location?
- Your profile shows to users within your selected discovery radius
- **Free users**: Can adjust radius but not change location
- **Premium users**: Can change location once per month (Passport mode)
- **Premium+ users**: Unlimited location changes
