## Premium Features Explained

### Unlimited Likes
**What it is:** Swipe right as many times as you want, every day.

**Why it matters:**
- Free users are limited to 50 likes per day
- If you're in a big city or picky about matches, 50 runs out quickly
- More likes = more potential matches = better chances of finding someone great

**Pro tip:** With unlimited likes, you can be more generous with right swipes and let the algorithm learn your preferences faster.

---

### See Who Liked You
**What it is:** View a list of everyone who has already liked your profile.

**Why it matters:**
- Instant matches - swipe right on people who already like you
- Save time - no guessing games
- Ego boost - see how popular you are
- Strategic swiping - focus on people already interested

**How it works:**
1. Go to the "Likes You" tab
2. Browse through profiles of people who liked you
3. Swipe right to match instantly or left to pass

---

### Advanced Filters
**What it is:** Filter potential matches by detailed criteria.

**Available Filters:**
- Height (exact range in feet/cm)
- Education level (High school, College, Graduate degree)
- Religion (Christianity, Islam, Judaism, Buddhism, Hinduism, Atheist, Agnostic, Other)
- Ethnicity (optional filter)
- Smoking preferences (Non-smoker, Social smoker, Regular smoker)
- Drinking preferences (Non-drinker, Social drinker, Regular drinker)
- Exercise frequency
- Children (Has kids, Doesn't have kids, Wants kids)

**Why it matters:**
- Find matches that align with your lifestyle and values
- Don't waste time on incompatible profiles
- More meaningful matches

---

### Rewind Feature
**What it is:** Undo your last swipe.

**How it works:**
- **Premium:** Tap the yellow rewind icon to undo your last swipe only
- **Premium+:** Unlimited rewinds - go back as many swipes as you want

**When to use it:**
- You accidentally swiped left on someone attractive
- You want to reconsider a profile
- Your finger slipped
- You changed your mind

---

### Read Receipts
**What it is:** See when your matches read your messages.

**What you'll see:**
- Sent: Message delivered
- Read: Message opened and read
- Timestamp of when they read it

**Why it matters:**
- Know if someone is interested or ignoring you
- Better timing for follow-up messages
- Set expectations appropriately

**Privacy:** This goes both ways - your matches can also see when you read their messages.

---

### Passport Mode
**What it is:** Change your location to match with people in other cities or countries.

**How it works:**
1. Go to Settings > Discovery Preferences
2. Tap "Passport"
3. Search for a city or country
4. Select your new location
5. Start swiping in that area

**Limits:**
- **Premium:** 1 location change per month
- **Premium+:** Unlimited location changes

**Great for:**
- Traveling and want to line up dates before you arrive
- Considering a move and want to see the dating scene
- Long-distance relationships
- Meeting people in your home country while abroad

---

### Profile Boost
**What it is:** Your profile becomes a top profile in your area for 30 minutes.

**Benefits:**
- 10x more profile views
- Increased match rate during boost
- Best used during peak hours (6-9 PM on weekdays, weekend afternoons)

**Included with Premium+:** 1 free boost per month
**Purchase separately:** $4.99 per boost

**Pro tips:**
- Update your profile before boosting
- Use during peak hours for maximum exposure
- Swipe during your boost to show you're active

---

### Spotlight
**What it is:** Be featured as the #1 profile in your area for 30 minutes.

**How it's different from Boost:**
- Boost = top profile in rotation
- Spotlight = THE top profile everyone sees
- Even better visibility than Boost

**Included with Premium+:** 1 free spotlight per month
**Purchase separately:** $4.99 per spotlight

---

### Incognito Mode (Premium+ Only)
**What it is:** Browse profiles invisibly.

**How it works:**
- Your profile is hidden from everyone
- You only appear to people you swipe right on
- Complete control over who sees you

**Great for:**
- Privacy-conscious users
- Public figures or professionals
- People in small communities who value discretion
- Users who want to browse without pressure

**Note:** You can toggle incognito on/off anytime in settings.

---

### Advanced Analytics (Premium+ Only)
**What it is:** Detailed stats about your profile performance.

**Metrics you'll see:**
- Profile views per day/week/month
- Like rate (% of people who swipe right on you)
- Match rate
- Message response rate
- Best performing photos
- Peak activity times
- Comparison to similar profiles in your area

**Why it matters:**
- Optimize your profile based on data
- Know what's working and what isn't
- Track improvement over time
- Make informed decisions about photos and bio

## How to Upgrade

### From the App

**iOS:**
1. Tap your profile icon (bottom right)
2. Tap the star icon or "Get Premium"
3. Choose your plan (Premium or Premium+)
4. Select monthly or yearly billing
5. Tap "Subscribe"
6. Confirm with Face ID/Touch ID/Password
7. Your premium features activate immediately

**Android:**
1. Tap your profile icon (bottom right)
2. Tap the star icon or "Get Premium"
3. Choose your plan (Premium or Premium+)
4. Select monthly or yearly billing
5. Tap "Subscribe"
6. Confirm your purchase
7. Your premium features activate immediately

**Web:**
1. Click your profile picture (top right)
2. Click "Upgrade to Premium"
3. Choose your plan
4. Enter payment details
5. Click "Subscribe Now"
6. Premium features activate immediately

### Payment Methods
- Credit/Debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover)
- Apple Pay (iOS)
- Google Pay (Android)
- PayPal (Web)

### Billing
- **Monthly subscriptions:** Billed every month on the date you subscribed
- **Yearly subscriptions:** Billed once per year, save up to 58%
- **Auto-renewal:** Automatically renews unless cancelled 24 hours before renewal
- **Invoices:** Emailed receipts for all charges

## Subscription Management

### How to Cancel

**iOS (Subscribed through Apple):**
1. Open iPhone Settings
2. Tap your name at the top
3. Tap "Subscriptions"
4. Find Flamoral
5. Tap "Cancel Subscription"
6. Confirm cancellation
7. Premium features remain active until the end of your billing period

**Android (Subscribed through Google Play):**
1. Open Google Play Store
2. Tap Menu > Subscriptions
3. Find Flamoral
4. Tap "Cancel Subscription"
5. Follow prompts to confirm
6. Premium features remain active until the end of your billing period

**Web (Subscribed through Flamoral.com):**
1. Go to Flamoral.com
2. Click Profile > Settings
3. Click "Subscription"
4. Click "Cancel Subscription"
5. Confirm cancellation
6. Premium features remain active until the end of your billing period

### How to Pause Subscription
Currently, subscriptions cannot be paused. You can:
- Cancel and resubscribe later (no penalty)
- Downgrade from Premium+ to Premium
- Switch from monthly to yearly or vice versa

### How to Change Subscription Tier
1. Go to Settings > Subscription
2. Tap "Change Plan"
3. Select new tier (Premium or Premium+)
4. Confirm change
5. If upgrading: Charged prorated difference immediately
6. If downgrading: Change takes effect at next billing cycle

### Refund Policy
- **7-day money-back guarantee** for first-time subscribers
- Refunds processed within 5-7 business days
- No refunds for partial months
- Contact support@flamoral.com for refund requests

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I switch between plans?
Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade anytime. Upgrades take effect immediately, downgrades take effect at your next billing cycle.

### What happens if I cancel?
Your premium features remain active until the end of your current billing period. After that, you'll revert to the free tier.

### Can I resubscribe after canceling?
Absolutely! You can resubscribe anytime at current pricing.

### Do yearly subscribers get charged monthly?
No, yearly subscribers are charged once per year at a discounted rate.

### Can I get a refund if I don't like Premium?
First-time subscribers have 7 days to request a full refund. Contact support@flamoral.com.

### Does Premium guarantee more matches?
Premium gives you tools to get more matches (unlimited likes, visibility boosts, etc.), but matches still depend on mutual interest and profile quality.

### Can I transfer my subscription to another account?
No, subscriptions are non-transferable.

### What happens to my purchased boosts/Super Likes if I cancel?
They remain in your account and can still be used after canceling your subscription.
