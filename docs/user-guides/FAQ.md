## Account Questions

### How do I create an account?
You can sign up using:
- Email and password
- Phone number (requires SMS verification)
- Google account
- Facebook account
- Apple ID (iOS only)

See our [Getting Started Guide](GETTING_STARTED.md) for detailed instructions.

### What's the minimum age to use Flamoral?
You must be 18 years or older to create a Flamoral account. We verify age during registration.

### Can I have multiple accounts?
No. Our Terms of Service prohibit multiple accounts. If we detect multiple accounts from the same person, all accounts may be banned.

### How do I change my email or phone number?
1. Go to Profile > Settings
2. Tap "Account Information"
3. Tap "Email" or "Phone Number"
4. Enter new information
5. Verify via link (email) or code (phone)

### How do I delete my account?
1. Go to Profile > Settings
2. Scroll to bottom and tap "Delete Account"
3. Select reason for leaving (optional)
4. Tap "Delete My Account"
5. Confirm deletion

**Warning:** Account deletion is permanent. All your data, matches, and messages will be deleted and cannot be recovered.

### Can I recover a deleted account?
No. Once you delete your account, all data is permanently removed from our systems after 30 days. You can create a new account, but it will start from scratch.

### I forgot my password. How do I reset it?
1. On the login screen, tap "Forgot Password?"
2. Enter your email address
3. Check your email for reset link
4. Click the link and create a new password
5. Log in with your new password

### How do I change my password?
1. Go to Profile > Settings
2. Tap "Password & Security"
3. Tap "Change Password"
4. Enter current password
5. Enter and confirm new password
6. Tap "Update Password"

### Can I pause my account instead of deleting it?
Not currently. You can either:
- Keep your account active (no cost for free users)
- Delete your account completely
- Disable notifications and stop using the app

## Matching Questions

### How does matching work?
Flamoral uses mutual matching:
1. You swipe right (like) on someone
2. They swipe right on you
3. It's a match! You can now message each other

### Why am I not getting matches?
Common reasons:
- **Incomplete profile**: Add more photos and complete your bio
- **Restrictive filters**: Expand your age range or distance
- **Not swiping enough**: Be more active to be shown to others
- **Profile quality**: Update photos and bio
- **Location**: Fewer users in your area

Tips to get more matches:
- Use 6-9 high-quality photos
- Write an engaging bio
- Verify your profile
- Swipe during peak hours (6-9 PM)
- Be active daily

### How many likes do I get per day?
- **Free users**: 50 likes per day
- **Premium users**: Unlimited likes
- **Premium+ users**: Unlimited likes

Likes reset at midnight in your local timezone.

### What's the difference between a like and a Super Like?
- **Like (swipe right)**: Standard way to show interest
- **Super Like (swipe up)**: Sends them a notification and shows you're really interested

Super Likes are limited:
- Free: 5 per week
- Premium: 5 per week
- Premium+: 10 per week

### Can I see who liked me?
- **Free users**: See blurred previews (can't see who)
- **Premium users**: See all profiles who liked you
- **Premium+** users: See all profiles who liked you

### Why did a match disappear?
Possible reasons:
- They unmatched you
- They deleted their account
- Their account was banned
- Technical glitch (rare)

### Can I rematch with someone I unmatched?
No. Once you unmatch someone, you won't see their profile again. This is permanent.

### How does the matching algorithm work?
Our algorithm considers:
- Your swipe history and preferences
- Location and distance settings
- Age range preferences
- Active users in your area
- Profile completeness and verification
- Mutual interests and compatibility factors

### What are Top Picks?
Top Picks are daily curated matches based on your preferences and swipe history. They refresh every 24 hours at noon.

### How far can I see profiles?
You can adjust your discovery radius in settings:
- Minimum: 1 mile (or 1 km)
- Maximum: 100 miles (or 160 km)

Premium+ users with Passport mode can change their location entirely.

## Privacy and Safety

### Is my location shared with other users?
No. Other users only see:
- Approximate distance (e.g., "5 miles away")
- Your city/area (not exact address)

Your precise location is never shared.

### Can I hide my profile?
- **Free users**: No
- **Premium+ users**: Yes, with Incognito Mode

Incognito mode hides your profile from everyone except people you swipe right on.

### How do I block someone?
1. Open their profile or conversation
2. Tap the three dots (menu icon)
3. Tap "Block User"
4. Confirm

Blocked users:
- Can't see your profile
- Can't message you
- Can't match with you
- Won't appear in your feed

### How do I report someone?
1. Open their profile or conversation
2. Tap the three dots (menu icon)
3. Tap "Report"
4. Select reason (inappropriate photos, harassment, scam, etc.)
5. Add details (optional)
6. Submit report

Our moderation team reviews all reports within 24 hours.

### What happens when I report someone?
- Our team reviews the report
- If violation confirmed, user may be warned, suspended, or banned
- You won't see their profile anymore
- You remain anonymous (they won't know who reported them)

### How does photo verification work?
1. Go to Profile Settings
2. Tap "Verify Your Profile"
3. Take a selfie following on-screen instructions
4. Our AI verifies it matches your profile photos
5. Get verified badge within 24 hours

Verified profiles get 40% more matches.

### Can I control who sees my profile?
**Free users:** Limited control (distance and age filters only)

**Premium+ users:** Full control with Incognito Mode
- Only appear to people you swipe right on
- Complete privacy

See our [Safety and Privacy Guide](SAFETY_AND_PRIVACY_GUIDE.md) for more details.

## Technical Issues

### The app keeps crashing. What should I do?
Try these steps:
1. Force close and restart the app
2. Update to the latest version
3. Restart your device
4. Clear app cache (Settings > Apps > Flamoral > Clear Cache)
5. Uninstall and reinstall the app

If issues persist, contact support@flamoral.com with your device model and OS version.

### I'm not receiving notifications.
**iOS:**
1. Settings > Notifications > Flamoral
2. Enable "Allow Notifications"
3. Select alert style

**Android:**
1. Settings > Apps > Flamoral > Notifications
2. Enable all notification types

**In-app:**
1. Flamoral > Profile > Settings > Notifications
2. Enable desired notification types

### Photos won't upload. Help!
Common solutions:
- Check internet connection
- Ensure photos are under 10MB
- Grant Flamoral permission to access photos
- Try a different photo format (JPG, PNG)
- Update the app
- Clear app cache

Supported formats: JPG, PNG, HEIC
Maximum size: 10MB per photo

### Messages aren't sending.
Check:
1. Internet connection (WiFi or cellular data)
2. App is updated to latest version
3. You haven't been blocked by the person
4. The match still exists (they haven't unmatched)

### The app is slow or laggy.
Try:
1. Close other apps running in background
2. Clear app cache
3. Update to latest version
4. Restart your device
5. Check internet connection speed

### I can't log in.
Common solutions:
- Verify email/password is correct
- Try "Forgot Password" to reset
- Check if account was deleted
- Ensure you're using the correct login method (email vs social)
- Update the app
- Try logging in on web (flamoral.com)

See our [Troubleshooting Guide](TROUBLESHOOTING_GUIDE.md) for more help.

## Profile and Settings

### How many photos can I upload?
- Minimum: 2 photos required
- Maximum: 9 photos
- Recommended: 6-9 photos for best results

### What makes a good profile photo?
Best practices:
- Clear face shots
- Good lighting
- Smiling and approachable
- Variety (close-up, full body, activities)
- Recent photos (within last year)
- No heavy filters

Avoid:
- Group photos where you can't be identified
- Sunglasses in every photo
- Heavily filtered or edited photos
- Photos with exes
- Inappropriate content

### Can I change my age or birthday?
No. Age/birthday cannot be changed after account creation to prevent fraud and ensure age verification integrity. If you entered it incorrectly, you must delete your account and create a new one.

### Can I change my gender or orientation preferences?
Yes, anytime:
1. Go to Profile > Settings
2. Tap "Discovery Preferences"
3. Update "Interested In"
4. Changes apply immediately

### How do I verify my profile?
1. Profile > Settings
2. Tap "Verify Your Profile"
3. Follow selfie instructions
4. Submit for review
5. Receive badge within 24 hours

Verification increases match rates by 40%.

### What are profile prompts?
Short questions you can answer to showcase personality:
- "The key to my heart is..."
- "I'm weirdly attracted to..."
- "My simple pleasures..."

Add 2-3 prompts in Profile > Edit Profile > Add Prompts

### Can I hide my profile from specific people?
No, there's no way to hide from specific users. Options:
- Block them (they won't see you)
- Use Incognito Mode (Premium+ only) to control who sees you

## Messaging

### Can I message someone before matching?
No. Flamoral requires mutual matching before messaging. This prevents spam and unwanted messages.

### Why can't I send photos in messages?
Check:
- Internet connection
- Photo size (under 10MB)
- Photo format (JPG, PNG)
- App permissions for photos
- App is updated

If problem persists, try:
- Different photo
- Restarting the app
- Reinstalling the app

### How do read receipts work?
**Premium and Premium+ users** can see:
- "Sent" - Message delivered
- "Read" - Message opened
- Timestamp of when read

**Free users**: Don't see read receipts

Note: This works both ways - if you have read receipts, others can see when you read their messages too.

### Can I send voice messages?
Yes! Tap the microphone icon in any conversation to record and send voice messages up to 60 seconds.

### How do I make a video call?
1. Open a conversation
2. Tap the video camera icon
3. Wait for them to accept
4. Enjoy your video chat

Video calls use Agora technology for high-quality, secure calls.

### Can I delete a sent message?
No. Messages cannot be deleted or edited after sending. Think before you send!

### How do I unmatch someone?
1. Open the conversation
2. Tap the three dots (menu)
3. Tap "Unmatch"
4. Confirm

Unmatching is permanent. You'll both lose the conversation and won't see each other again.

### What's an icebreaker?
Icebreakers are suggested conversation starters that appear when you match. Examples:
- "I see you like hiking! What's your favorite trail?"
- "Great photo from Japan! How long were you there?"

They're based on profile information and help start conversations.
