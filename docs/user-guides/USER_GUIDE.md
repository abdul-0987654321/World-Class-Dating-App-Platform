# ConnectSphere User Guide

## Getting Started

### 1. Download the App
- **iOS**: Download from App Store
- **Android**: Download from Google Play Store
- **Web**: Visit https://connectsphere.com

### 2. Create Your Account
1. Enter your email address
2. Create a secure password
3. Verify your phone number (SMS code)
4. Accept Terms of Service and Privacy Policy

### 3. Complete Your Profile
1. Upload 2-9 photos (minimum 2 required)
2. Write your bio (250 characters max)
3. Add your interests (select 3-10)
4. Fill in lifestyle preferences
5. Set your discovery preferences

## Finding Matches

### Swiping
- **Swipe Right** or tap ❤️ to Like
- **Swipe Left** or tap ✖️ to Pass
- **Swipe Up** or tap ⭐ to Super Like
- **Tap card** to view full profile

### Daily Limits (Free Users)
- 50 regular likes per day
- 1 super like per day
- Resets at midnight

### Filters
**Basic (Free)**:
- Age range
- Maximum distance

**Advanced (Premium)**:
- Height
- Education
- Religion
- Lifestyle (smoking, drinking, exercise)

## Messaging

### Starting a Conversation
1. Wait for a mutual match
2. Get match notification
3. Start chatting instantly
4. Use icebreaker questions for help

### Message Features
- Send text messages
- Share photos
- Send voice messages
- React with emoji
- Video/voice calls

## Premium Features

### Premium ($19.99/month)
- Unlimited likes
- See who liked you

- 5 Super Likes per week
- Rewind last swipe
- Read receipts

### Premium+ ($29.99/month)
- All Premium features
- Profile boost (monthly)
- Incognito mode
- Unlimited rewinds
- 10 Super Likes per week
- Priority support

## Safety Tips

1. **Never share personal info** in early messages
2. **Meet in public places** for first dates
3. **Tell a friend** where you're going
4. **Trust your instincts** - report suspicious behavior
5. **Video chat first** before meeting in person
6. **Report inappropriate behavior** immediately

## Reporting & Blocking

### To Report a User:
1. Open their profile
2. Tap menu (•••)
3. Select "Report"
4. Choose reason and submit

### To Block a User:
1. Open their profile
2. Tap menu (•••)
3. Select "Block"
4. Confirm

## Account Settings

### Privacy Controls
- Hide your distance
- Incognito mode (Premium+)
- Pause your account
- Limit who can see you

### Notifications
- New matches
- New messages
- Profile likes
- Daily picks

### Account Deletion
Settings > Account > Delete Account
- All data permanently deleted within 30 days
- Cannot be undone

## Support
