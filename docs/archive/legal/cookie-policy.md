## 1. What Are Cookies?

### 1.1 Definition

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device (computer, smartphone, tablet) when you visit a website or use an application. Cookies help websites recognize your device and remember information about your visit.

### 1.2 How Cookies Work

When you visit our Service:
1. Your browser or app requests a page from our server
2. Our server sends the page along with a cookie
3. Your browser stores the cookie on your device
4. When you visit again, your browser sends the cookie back to our server
5. The server reads the cookie and can personalize your experience

### 1.3 Cookie Information

Cookies may contain information such as:
- A unique identifier
- The website domain
- Expiration date
- Session or persistent status
- User preferences and settings
- Login status
- Device and browser information

## 2. Types of Cookies We Use

### 2.1 Classification by Purpose

We use different types of cookies for various purposes:

#### Essential Cookies (Strictly Necessary)

**Purpose:** Required for the Service to function properly

**What they do:**
- Enable account login and authentication
- Remember items in your shopping cart (for subscriptions)
- Maintain security and prevent fraud
- Enable core functionality of the Service
- Load balance across servers

**Can you disable them?** No. These cookies are essential for the Service to work. Disabling them will prevent you from using key features.

**Examples:**
- Session authentication cookies
- Security tokens
- Load balancing cookies

#### Preference Cookies (Functionality Cookies)

**Purpose:** Remember your settings and preferences

**What they do:**
- Remember your language preference
- Remember your location settings
- Store your notification preferences
- Remember your display settings (dark mode, etc.)
- Save your filter and search preferences

**Can you disable them?** Yes, but this may affect your experience. You'll need to reset preferences each time you visit.

**Examples:**
- Language preference cookie
- Theme preference (light/dark mode)
- Filter settings

#### Analytics and Performance Cookies

**Purpose:** Help us understand how users interact with the Service

**What they do:**
- Collect statistics about how you use the Service
- Measure which features are most popular
- Identify technical issues and errors
- Track page load times and performance
- Understand user flow through the Service
- Analyze conversion rates

**Can you disable them?** Yes. We'll still collect some analytics data, but it will be less detailed.

**Examples:**
- Google Analytics cookies
- Firebase Analytics (mobile)
- Custom analytics cookies
- A/B testing cookies

**Data collected:**
- Pages visited
- Time spent on pages
- Click patterns
- Error messages
- Device and browser type
- Approximate location (city/country)

#### Advertising and Marketing Cookies

**Purpose:** Deliver relevant advertisements and measure their effectiveness

**What they do:**
- Show you personalized ads based on your interests
- Limit how many times you see the same ad
- Measure ad campaign effectiveness
- Track conversions from ads
- Enable retargeting (showing ads for Flamoral on other sites)
- Share data with advertising partners

**Can you disable them?** Yes. You'll still see ads, but they won't be personalized to your interests.

**Examples:**
- Facebook Pixel
- Google Ads conversion tracking
- Advertising network cookies
- Retargeting cookies

### 2.2 Classification by Duration

#### Session Cookies

- **Duration:** Temporary; deleted when you close your browser or app
- **Purpose:** Remember your actions during a single browsing session
- **Examples:** Login session, items in cart, form data

#### Persistent Cookies

- **Duration:** Remain on your device for a set period or until manually deleted
- **Purpose:** Remember your preferences across multiple sessions
- **Examples:** "Remember me" login, language preference, theme settings
- **Typical duration:** 30 days to 2 years, depending on purpose

### 2.3 Classification by Source

#### First-Party Cookies

- **Set by:** Flamoral (our domain)
- **Purpose:** Enable core functionality and collect analytics
- **Control:** Managed through our cookie settings

#### Third-Party Cookies

- **Set by:** External services integrated with our Service
- **Purpose:** Analytics, advertising, social media features
- **Control:** Managed through our cookie settings and third-party opt-out tools

**Third parties we work with:**
- Google (Analytics, Ads, Firebase)
- Facebook (Pixel, Social Login)
- Advertising networks
- Payment processors
- Analytics providers

## 3. Other Tracking Technologies

### 3.1 Web Beacons (Pixel Tags)

**What they are:** Small, invisible graphic images embedded in web pages or emails

**What they do:**
- Track whether you've opened an email
- Monitor which parts of a page you've viewed
- Work in conjunction with cookies to collect analytics
- Enable ad conversion tracking

**Where we use them:**
- Email newsletters
- Marketing emails
- Service notifications
- Website pages
- Online advertisements

### 3.2 Local Storage

**What it is:** HTML5 technology that stores data in your browser

**What we use it for:**
- Store larger amounts of data than cookies allow
- Cache resources for faster page loading
- Store application state
- Save user preferences

**Types:**
- LocalStorage (persistent)
- SessionStorage (temporary)

**Data stored:**
- UI preferences
- Cached profile data
- App configuration
- Temporary form data

### 3.3 Mobile SDKs (Software Development Kits)

**What they are:** Code libraries embedded in mobile applications

**What they do:**
- Collect analytics about app usage
- Enable push notifications
- Track crashes and errors
- Facilitate advertising

**Examples:**
- Google Firebase
- Facebook SDK
- AppsFlyer
- Mixpanel

**Data collected:**
- Device identifier
- App usage statistics
- Device type and OS version
- Crash reports

### 3.4 Device Fingerprinting

**What it is:** Technique to identify devices based on configuration and settings

**What we use it for:**
- Fraud prevention
- Security
- Account protection

**Note:** We use limited fingerprinting primarily for security purposes, not for tracking across websites.

## 6. Managing Cookies

You have several options to control and manage cookies.

### 6.1 Cookie Preference Center

**Access our Cookie Preference Center:**
- Website: Click "Cookie Settings" in the footer
- Mobile App: Settings > Privacy > Cookie Preferences
- Direct link: www.flamoral.com/cookie-settings

**Choose your preferences:**
- Essential Cookies: Cannot be disabled
- Preference Cookies: Toggle on/off
- Analytics Cookies: Toggle on/off
- Advertising Cookies: Toggle on/off

**Save your choices:**
- Your preferences are saved in a cookie
- They apply across all your devices where you're logged in
- You can change them anytime

### 6.2 Browser Settings

All modern browsers allow you to manage cookies through settings.

#### Google Chrome

1. Settings > Privacy and security > Cookies and other site data
2. Choose: "Allow all cookies," "Block third-party cookies," or "Block all cookies"
3. Manage exceptions and clear cookies

#### Mozilla Firefox

1. Settings > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data
2. Choose cookie acceptance settings
3. Manage exceptions and clear data

#### Safari (Desktop)

1. Preferences > Privacy
2. Choose "Block all cookies" or manage individual cookies
3. Use "Manage Website Data" to view and remove cookies

#### Safari (iOS)

1. Settings > Safari > Privacy & Security
2. Enable "Prevent Cross-Site Tracking"
3. Enable "Block All Cookies" (may affect functionality)

#### Microsoft Edge

1. Settings > Privacy, search, and services > Cookies and site permissions
2. Manage and delete cookies
3. Block third-party cookies

#### Opera

1. Settings > Privacy & security > Cookies
2. Choose cookie preferences
3. Clear browsing data

### 6.3 Browser Extensions

Install browser extensions to manage cookies:
- **Ghostery:** Block trackers and cookies
- **Privacy Badger:** Automatically block advertising trackers
- **Cookie AutoDelete:** Automatically delete cookies when you close a tab
- **uBlock Origin:** Block ads and trackers

### 6.4 Do Not Track (DNT)

Some browsers offer a "Do Not Track" setting that sends a signal to websites requesting not to be tracked.

**Current status:** There is no universal standard for DNT, and we do not currently respond to DNT signals. However, you can use our Cookie Preference Center to opt out of tracking.

### 6.5 Mobile App Settings

#### iOS

**Limit Ad Tracking:**
1. Settings > Privacy > Advertising
2. Enable "Limit Ad Tracking"

**Reset Advertising Identifier:**
1. Settings > Privacy > Advertising
2. Tap "Reset Advertising Identifier"

#### Android

**Opt out of Ad Personalization:**
1. Settings > Google > Ads
2. Enable "Opt out of Ads Personalization"

**Reset Advertising ID:**
1. Settings > Google > Ads
2. Tap "Reset advertising ID"

### 6.6 Third-Party Opt-Out Tools

Opt out of interest-based advertising from third parties:

**Network Advertising Initiative (NAI):**
- Website: optout.networkadvertising.org
- Opt out of multiple advertising networks at once

**Digital Advertising Alliance (DAA):**
- Website: optout.aboutads.info
- Choose companies to opt out from

**European Interactive Digital Advertising Alliance (EDAA):**
- Website: youronlinechoices.eu
- For users in Europe

**Google Ads Settings:**
- Website: adssettings.google.com
- Manage Google ad personalization

**Facebook Ad Preferences:**
- Website: facebook.com/ads/preferences
- Control Facebook ads on and off Facebook

## 7. Consequences of Disabling Cookies

### 7.1 Essential Cookies

If you block essential cookies:
- **Cannot log in** to your account
- **Cannot use core features** of the Service
- **Security may be compromised**
- **Payment processing won't work**

**Recommendation:** Keep essential cookies enabled

### 7.2 Preference Cookies

If you block preference cookies:
- Settings reset each visit
- Language preference not saved
- Search filters not remembered
- Reduced user experience

**Impact:** Moderate inconvenience

### 7.3 Analytics Cookies

If you block analytics cookies:
- No impact on functionality
- We'll have less data to improve the Service
- Generic experience (less personalized)

**Impact:** Minimal

### 7.4 Advertising Cookies

If you block advertising cookies:
- You'll still see ads (same number)
- Ads won't be personalized to your interests
- May see less relevant ads
- Ads may be repeated more often

**Impact:** Minimal to moderate

## 9. Data Protection and Security

### 9.1 Cookie Data Security

We protect cookie data through:
- **Encryption:** Sensitive cookies are encrypted
- **Secure flag:** Cookies transmitted only over HTTPS
- **HttpOnly flag:** Prevents JavaScript access to sensitive cookies

### 9.2 Data Retention

**Session cookies:** Deleted when you close your browser
**Persistent cookies:** Expire after the duration specified
**Analytics data:** Retained for up to 26 months (Google Analytics)
