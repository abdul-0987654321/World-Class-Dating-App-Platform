## 4. Your Rights and Choices

You have certain rights regarding your personal information, which may vary depending on your location.

### 4.1 Access and Portability

**Right to Access:**
You have the right to request access to the personal information we hold about you. You can access most of your information through your account settings.

**Right to Data Portability:**
You have the right to receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format. You can request a copy of your data by contacting us at privacy@flamoral.com.

### 4.2 Correction and Deletion

**Right to Correct:**
You can update and correct your profile information at any time through your account settings.

**Right to Delete (Right to be Forgotten):**
You have the right to request deletion of your personal information. You can delete your account through:
- Account settings > Delete Account
- Emailing privacy@flamoral.com

Please note:
- Some information may be retained for legal or legitimate business purposes
- Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone
- Backup copies may take up to 90 days to delete
- We may retain transaction records for tax and accounting purposes

### 4.3 Objection and Restriction

**Right to Object:**
You have the right to object to certain uses of your information, including:
- Direct marketing (you can opt out at any time)
- Processing based on legitimate interests

**Right to Restrict Processing:**
You have the right to request that we limit how we use your information in certain circumstances.

### 4.4 Communication Preferences

**Email Communications:**
You can opt out of promotional emails by:
- Clicking "unsubscribe" in the email
- Adjusting notification settings in your account
- Contacting privacy@flamoral.com

Note: You cannot opt out of service-related communications (account notifications, security alerts, etc.).

**Push Notifications:**
You can disable push notifications through:
- Device settings
- App notification settings

**SMS Messages:**
You can opt out of SMS messages by replying "STOP" or adjusting your account settings.

### 4.5 Cookie Preferences

You can control cookie settings through:
- Your browser settings
- Our cookie preference center
- Third-party opt-out tools

Note: Disabling certain cookies may affect Service functionality.

### 4.6 Do Not Track

We currently do not respond to "Do Not Track" signals because there is no industry standard for compliance.

### 4.7 Exercising Your Rights

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at:
- Email: privacy@flamoral.com
- Account Settings: Privacy & Data section

We will respond to your request within:
- **30 days** for general requests
- **45 days** for CCPA requests (with possible 45-day extension)
- **1 month** for GDPR requests (with possible 2-month extension for complex requests)

We may require identity verification before processing your request.
