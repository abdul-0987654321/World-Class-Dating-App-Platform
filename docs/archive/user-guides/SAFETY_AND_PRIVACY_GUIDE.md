## Safety Features

### Profile Verification

**What it is:** Get a verified badge to show you're a real person.

**How it works:**
1. Go to Profile > Settings
2. Tap "Verify Your Profile"
3. Take a selfie following on-screen instructions
4. Our AI compares it to your profile photos
5. Get your verification badge within 24 hours

**Benefits:**
- Verified profiles get 40% more matches
- Shows you're trustworthy
- Helps combat catfishing and fake profiles
- Increases user confidence

**Pro tip:** Only match with verified profiles for extra safety.

- Ensure clear view of face

**What it means:**
- Blue checkmark on profile
- AI-verified to match profile photos
- Real person, not catfish
- Trustworthy account

**Maintaining verification:**
- Update verification if appearance changes significantly
- Don't change all your photos drastically
- Re-verify every 6 months (automatic prompt)

## Block and Report

### When to Block Someone

**Block if they:**
- Make you uncomfortable
- Are harassing you
- Send inappropriate content
- Won't respect your boundaries
- Are being disrespectful

**Blocking is private** - they won't be notified.

### How to Block

1. Open their profile or conversation
2. Tap menu (three dots)
3. Tap "Block [Name]"
4. Confirm block

**What happens when you block:**
- They can't see your profile
- They can't message you
- Conversation disappears for both
- They won't appear in your feed
- They can't rematch with you

### Unblocking Someone

1. Profile > Settings > Privacy
2. Tap "Blocked Users"
3. Find user and tap "Unblock"

**Note:** Unblocking doesn't restore match or messages.

---

### When to Report Someone

**Report for:**
- Harassment or bullying
- Inappropriate photos or messages
- Scam or fraud attempts
- Underage user (under 18)
- Violent or hateful content
- Spam
- Fake profile or catfishing
- Sexual content or solicitation
- Threats or safety concerns

**Your report is anonymous.**

### How to Report

1. Open their profile or conversation
2. Tap menu (three dots)
3. Tap "Report"
4. Select reason:
   - Inappropriate photos
   - Inappropriate messages
   - Harassment
   - Scam/fraud
   - Underage
   - Fake profile
   - Hate speech
   - Threats
   - Other
5. Add details (optional but helpful)
6. Submit report

**What happens after reporting:**
- Our moderation team reviews within 24 hours
- User may be warned, suspended, or banned
- You're automatically blocked from them
- You won't see their profile again
- If violation confirmed, action taken against their account

### Report Outcomes

**Possible actions:**
- **Warning**: First-time minor violations
- **Temporary suspension**: 7-30 days
- **Permanent ban**: Serious violations, repeat offenders
- **No action**: If no violation found

**You'll be notified of outcome within 48 hours.**

## Safe Dating Tips

### Before Meeting

**1. Video chat first**
- Use Flamoral's built-in video call
- Verify they match their photos
- Assess genuine connection
- No need to share phone number yet

**2. Research your date**
- Google their name
- Reverse image search their photos
- Check social media profiles (if available)
- Trust your instincts

**3. Share your plans**
- Tell a friend:
  - Who you're meeting (name, profile screenshot)
  - Where you're going
  - What time (start and expected end)
- Use "Share My Date" feature in app
- Check in with friend during/after

**4. Choose safe location**
- **Public place** (restaurant, coffee shop, park)
- **Daytime** for first meeting
- **Familiar area** you know well
- **Easy exit** if needed

**5. Plan your transportation**
- Drive yourself or use rideshare
- Don't let them pick you up at home
- Have your own way to leave
- Don't rely on them for transportation

---

### During the Date

**Stay safe:**
1. **Stay in public** - Don't go to their place or invite them to yours on first date
2. **Watch your drink** - Never leave drink unattended, get new one if you do
3. **Moderate alcohol** - Stay in control, limit drinks
4. **Keep phone charged** - Ensure you can call for help or rideshare
5. **Trust your gut** - If something feels off, it probably is
6. **Have exit strategy** - Pre-arranged "emergency" call from friend
7. **Don't share too much** - Keep personal details private (address, workplace specifics)

**Red flags to watch for:**
- Pressuring you to drink more
- Suggesting isolated locations
- Asking invasive personal questions
- Disrespecting your boundaries
- Inconsistencies in their story
- Aggressive or controlling behavior
- Asking for money
- Rushing intimacy

**If you feel unsafe:**
- Excuse yourself to restroom, call friend/rideshare
- Tell staff if you need help
- Leave immediately - don't worry about being rude
- Use emergency contact feature in app
- Call 911 if necessary

---

### After the Date

**Stay safe:**
1. **Don't invite them home** (or go to theirs) on first date
2. **Text friend when home** to confirm safety
3. **Trust your feelings** about second date
4. **Block if uncomfortable** - no explanation needed
5. **Report concerning behavior** to Flamoral

---

### Online Safety

**Protect your identity:**

**Don't share too early:**
- Full name (first name only until ready)
- Phone number (use in-app calling)
- Email address
- Home/work address
- Social media handles (until you trust them)
- Financial information (never!)

**Keep conversations on Flamoral initially:**
- End-to-end encrypted messages
- Can report/block easily

**Be wary of:**
- Requests to move off-platform immediately
- Requests for money or financial help
- Sob stories or emergencies requiring money
- Offers that seem too good to be true
- Love bombing (excessive flattery early on)
- Refusal to video chat or meet in person

## Recognizing Red Flags

### Profile Red Flags

**Be cautious if:**
- Only 1-2 photos (or all look professional/model-like)
- Photos look like stock images or celebrities
- Vague or empty bio
- Inconsistent information
- Photos don't match (different people)
- All photos are group shots (can't identify them)
- Overly sexual or provocative content
- Extreme wealth displayed
- Not verified

**Check:**
- Reverse image search their photos
- Look for verification badge
- Check if profile seems genuine

---

### Conversation Red Flags

**Warning signs:**
- **Moves too fast**: "I love you" within days
- **Love bombing**: Excessive compliments and affection
- **Avoids video chat**: Always has excuses
- **Sob stories**: Emergencies, sick relatives, hardships
- **Asks for money**: For any reason - NEVER send money
- **Asks for personal info immediately**: Address, workplace, etc.
- **Inconsistent stories**: Details don't match previous conversations
- **Pressure**: To meet immediately, share photos, move off app
- **Sexual content immediately**: Explicit messages or photos
- **Refuses to meet**: After weeks of chatting

---

### Common Scams

**1. Romance scams:**
- Build emotional connection quickly
- Create crisis requiring money
- Always have excuse why they can't meet/video chat
- May send gifts to seem legitimate
- Eventually ask for money (emergency, travel, medical, etc.)

**Prevention:** Never send money to someone you haven't met in person.

**2. Catfishing:**
- Use fake photos and identity
- Build relationship under false pretenses
- May eventually ask for money or manipulate
- Refuse to video chat

**Prevention:** Video chat early, reverse image search photos, verify profile.

**3. Phishing/scams:**
- Send links to "verify" your account
- Ask you to sign up for other sites
- Request personal information
- Try to get credit card info

**Prevention:** Never click suspicious links, we'll never ask for payment info via message.

**4. Crypto/investment scams:**
- Introduce cryptocurrency or investment opportunities
- Promise unrealistic returns
- Pressure you to invest
- Eventually disappear with your money

**Prevention:** Don't discuss or accept financial advice from matches.
