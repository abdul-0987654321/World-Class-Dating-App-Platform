Community Guidelines

Flamoral is a community built on respect, authenticity, and genuine connections. These guidelines help create a safe and welcoming environment for everyone.

Be Authentic

  • Do: Use recent photos that accurately represent you
  • Do: Be honest in your profile and conversations
  • Don't: Use fake photos or impersonate others
  • Don't: Create multiple accounts

Be Respectful

  • Do: Treat others the way you want to be treated
  • Do: Accept rejection gracefully
  • Don't: Send harassing or threatening messages
  • Don't: Discriminate based on race, religion, gender, or sexuality
  • Don't: Body shame or insult others

Keep It Safe

  • Do: Report suspicious or harmful behavior
  • Do: Meet in public places for first dates
  • Do: Tell someone where you're going
  • Don't: Share personal information too quickly
  • Don't: Send money to people you haven't met

Keep It Clean

  • Do: Keep conversations appropriate
  • Don't: Send unsolicited explicit content
  • Don't: Post nudity in your profile photos
  • Don't: Use sexually explicit language in your bio

No Spam or Scams

  • Don't: Promote products, services, or websites
  • Don't: Solicit money from other users
  • Don't: Send repetitive or automated messages
  • Don't: Attempt to scam or defraud others

No Illegal Activity

  • Don't: Use Flamoral for illegal purposes
  • Don't: Promote or sell illegal substances
  • Don't: Engage in or solicit prostitution
  • Don't: Share content involving minors

Consequences

Violations of these guidelines may result in:

  • Warning
  • Temporary suspension
  • Permanent ban
  • Report to law enforcement (for serious violations)

Reporting

If you encounter someone violating these guidelines, please report them:

  • Tap the three dots on their profile
  • Select "Report"
  • Choose the reason and add details

All reports are reviewed by our team. We take your safety seriously.
