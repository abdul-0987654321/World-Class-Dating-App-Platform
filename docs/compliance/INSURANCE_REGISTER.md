## Overview

This Insurance Register documents all corporate insurance policies maintained by the organization to protect against operational, legal, and cyber risks inherent to operating a dating platform. This register serves as the central repository for policy information, renewal tracking, and claims management.

### Purpose

- Maintain a comprehensive record of all insurance coverage
- Ensure adequate protection against platform-specific risks
- Track policy renewals and prevent coverage gaps
- Provide clear procedures for claims and COI requests
- Support compliance and audit requirements

### Scope

This register covers all insurance policies held at the corporate level. It does not cover employee personal insurance benefits (health, dental, vision) which are managed separately by Human Resources.

## Required Insurance Types

The following insurance types are required for operating a dating platform that handles sensitive personal data and facilitates interpersonal connections.

### 1. Cyber Liability Insurance

**Purpose:** Protects against losses resulting from data breaches, cyber attacks, ransomware, and other digital security incidents.

**Key Coverage Areas:**
- Data breach response and notification costs
- Ransomware extortion payments and recovery
- Business interruption from cyber events
- Regulatory fines and penalties
- Third-party claims from data exposure
- Forensic investigation costs
- Credit monitoring services for affected users
- Public relations and crisis management

**Platform-Specific Considerations:**
- Dating platforms store highly sensitive personal data including photos, location data, sexual orientation, and relationship preferences
- User data breaches can cause significant personal harm and reputational damage
- Platform must protect against unauthorized profile access and identity theft
- Real-time messaging systems are attractive targets for malicious actors

---

### 2. Errors & Omissions (E&O) / Professional Liability Insurance

**Purpose:** Covers claims arising from professional services, including negligence, misrepresentation, or failure to deliver promised services.

**Key Coverage Areas:**
- Negligence in service delivery
- Failure to perform professional duties
- Unintentional misrepresentation
- Violation of good faith and fair dealing
- Breach of contract claims
- Defense costs for covered claims

**Platform-Specific Considerations:**
- Matching algorithm failures or misrepresentations
- Safety feature failures (background checks, verification)
- Profile verification errors
- Premium subscription service failures
- API service level agreement breaches

---

### 3. General Liability Insurance

**Purpose:** Covers third-party bodily injury, property damage, and personal/advertising injury claims.

**Key Coverage Areas:**
- Third-party bodily injury
- Property damage
- Personal and advertising injury
- Medical payments
- Legal defense costs
- Premises liability (for any physical office locations)

**Platform-Specific Considerations:**
- Incidents at company-sponsored events or meetups
- Claims related to advertising and marketing activities
- Office visitor injuries
- Product liability for any physical merchandise

---

### 4. Directors & Officers (D&O) Insurance

**Purpose:** Protects company leadership from personal liability arising from decisions made in their official capacity.

**Key Coverage Areas:**
- Wrongful act claims against directors and officers
- Shareholder derivative actions
- Securities claims
- Employment practices claims against leadership
- Regulatory investigations
- Defense costs and settlements

**Platform-Specific Considerations:**
- Investor disputes regarding platform safety decisions
- Regulatory actions related to user data handling
- Decisions related to content moderation policies
- Platform safety and trust decisions

---

### 5. Employment Practices Liability Insurance (EPLI)

**Purpose:** Covers claims made by employees alleging discrimination, harassment, wrongful termination, or other employment-related issues.

**Key Coverage Areas:**
- Discrimination claims (age, race, gender, disability, etc.)
- Sexual harassment claims
- Wrongful termination
- Retaliation claims
- Failure to promote
- Wage and hour disputes
- Workplace bullying
- Negligent hiring/supervision

**Platform-Specific Considerations:**
- Tech industry faces heightened scrutiny on workplace culture
- Remote work arrangements create unique employment risks
- Content moderation team exposure to harmful content
- Contractor vs. employee classification risks

## Minimum Recommended Coverage Limits

Based on industry standards for dating platforms handling sensitive personal data, the following minimum coverage limits are recommended:

| Insurance Type | Minimum Coverage | Rationale |
|---------------|------------------|-----------|
| **Cyber Liability** | $5,000,000 aggregate | High volume of sensitive personal data including photos, location, preferences |
| **Errors & Omissions (E&O)** | $2,000,000 per occurrence | Professional service claims can be substantial; matching/safety feature risks |
| **General Liability** | $1,000,000 per occurrence | Standard commercial coverage; adjust based on office locations and events |
| **Directors & Officers (D&O)** | $2,000,000 aggregate | Investor protection; regulatory exposure in dating/social space |
| **Employment Practices (EPLI)** | $1,000,000 aggregate | Workforce size dependent; content moderation team risks |

### Coverage Limit Review Triggers

Coverage limits should be reviewed and potentially increased when:
- User base exceeds 1 million active users
- Annual revenue exceeds $10 million
- Expanding to new geographic markets (especially EU, California)
- Adding new data-intensive features (video calling, AI matching)
- Completing funding rounds or preparing for IPO
- Following any significant incident or claim

## Annual Review Checklist

The insurance portfolio must be reviewed annually, with the review completed at least 90 days before the earliest policy renewal date.

### Coverage Adequacy Review

- [ ] Review user growth and data volume changes
- [ ] Assess new features launched that may create additional risk exposure
- [ ] Evaluate geographic expansion (new jurisdictions = new regulations)
- [ ] Review industry benchmark coverage levels
- [ ] Analyze any regulatory changes affecting required coverage
- [ ] Assess changes in company valuation or funding status
- [ ] Review any incidents that may indicate coverage gaps
- [ ] Evaluate contractor/vendor insurance requirements
- [ ] Confirm coverage limits meet contractual obligations

### Premium Comparison

- [ ] Obtain renewal quotes from current carriers
- [ ] Request competitive quotes from at least 2 alternative carriers
- [ ] Compare coverage terms, not just premiums
- [ ] Evaluate carrier financial stability (AM Best ratings)
- [ ] Review carrier claims handling reputation
- [ ] Assess bundling opportunities for premium savings
- [ ] Consider deductible adjustments for premium optimization
- [ ] Document premium comparison analysis

### Claim History Review

- [ ] Compile list of all claims filed in past 12 months
- [ ] Review claim outcomes and settlements
- [ ] Identify any claim trends or patterns
- [ ] Assess impact of claims on renewal premiums
- [ ] Document lessons learned from claims
- [ ] Update risk mitigation strategies based on claim history
- [ ] Review reserved but unpaid claims

### Policy Update Requirements

- [ ] Update named insureds if corporate structure changed
- [ ] Add/remove additional insured parties as needed
- [ ] Update office locations and addresses
- [ ] Revise revenue and employee count projections
- [ ] Update technology stack and security certifications
- [ ] Review and update policy endorsements
- [ ] Confirm notification requirements are understood
- [ ] Update broker and carrier contact information

## Claim Filing Procedures

### Immediate Response (Within 24 Hours of Incident)

1. **Identify the Incident Type**
   - Determine which insurance policy(ies) may be triggered
   - Cyber incidents: Contact CISO immediately
   - Employment matters: Contact HR and Legal immediately
   - General liability: Contact Legal immediately

2. **Preserve Evidence**
   - Do not delete or modify any relevant records
   - Document the incident timeline
   - Preserve all communications
   - Take screenshots if applicable
   - Secure any physical evidence

3. **Internal Notification**
   - Notify Legal Department immediately
   - Notify CFO/Finance
   - Notify CEO for significant incidents
   - Document who was notified and when

### Claim Submission Process (Within 72 Hours)

1. **Contact Insurance Broker**
   - Primary Contact: [TO BE COMPLETED]
   - Phone: [TO BE COMPLETED]
   - Email: [TO BE COMPLETED]
   - After-hours emergency: [TO BE COMPLETED]

2. **Gather Required Information**
   - Incident date and time
   - Detailed description of what occurred
   - Parties involved (internal and external)
   - Potential damages or losses
   - Actions taken to mitigate
   - Relevant documentation and evidence

3. **Complete Claim Forms**
   - Obtain claim forms from broker or carrier
   - Complete all sections thoroughly
   - Attach supporting documentation
   - Have Legal review before submission
   - Keep copies of everything submitted

4. **Submit Claim**
   - Submit via method specified by carrier
   - Obtain confirmation of receipt
   - Note claim number assigned
   - Document submission date and method

### Post-Submission Management

1. **Claim Tracking**
   - Maintain claim file with all correspondence
   - Track claim status and adjuster assignment
   - Document all phone calls and meetings
   - Meet all carrier deadlines and requests

2. **Cooperation with Investigation**
   - Respond promptly to adjuster requests
   - Provide additional documentation as needed
   - Make personnel available for interviews
   - Do not admit liability without carrier approval

3. **Settlement Review**
   - Review any settlement offers with Legal
   - Ensure settlement adequately addresses damages
   - Understand any release terms before signing
   - Document final resolution

## Certificate of Insurance (COI) Request Process

### When COIs Are Required

Certificates of Insurance are typically requested by:
- Enterprise clients and partners
- Landlords and property managers
- Event venues
- Contractors and vendors (for mutual verification)
- Investors during due diligence
- Regulatory bodies

### Standard COI Request Process

1. **Receive Request**
   - Obtain specific requirements from requesting party
   - Note any additional insured requirements
   - Identify deadline for providing certificate
   - Document special coverage requirements

2. **Submit Request to Broker**
   - Email: [TO BE COMPLETED]
   - Include: Requesting party name and address
   - Include: Specific coverage types needed on certificate
   - Include: Additional insured requirements (if any)
   - Include: Deadline for certificate delivery
   - Include: Any special endorsements required

3. **Processing Time**
   - Standard requests: 2-3 business days
   - Rush requests: 24-48 hours (may incur fee)
   - Complex requests (additional insured): 3-5 business days

4. **Review and Distribute**
   - Review certificate for accuracy
   - Ensure all requested coverages are listed
   - Verify additional insured status if required
   - Send certificate to requesting party
   - File copy in COI register
